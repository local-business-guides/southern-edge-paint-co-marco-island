A short checklist for your painting plans

Before choosing a painter, make a quick list of questions and details:

  • Which rooms or exterior areas are part of the project?
  • What preparation is needed before painting begins?
  • Are there surfaces or repairs you want discussed separately?
  • What finish are you hoping to see, and where?
  • How should furniture, belongings, or access to the work area be handled?

You can prepare by clearing small items from the areas you want painted and pointing out any concerns you’ve noticed. Don’t assume a particular surface or room is included unless it has been discussed. Skipping that conversation can lead to different expectations about the scope or the final appearance. If you’re unsure how much of the home to include, ask a painter to talk through your options.
