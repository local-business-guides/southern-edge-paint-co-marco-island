How to get ready for a repaint

Once you've identified the project areas, think about access and day-to-day routines. For interior painting, put away breakable items and move personal belongings that are close to the surfaces being painted. If you have questions about larger furniture or other items, raise them before work begins so you know what to plan for.

For exterior painting, make a simple list of the sides or features you want to discuss. Ask how surface preparation will be approached and confirm the parts of the home included in the job. A common mistake is to assume a nearby surface or detail is automatically covered. Confirm the boundaries of the work instead.

You can also ask how updates will be shared if a question comes up. Clear communication helps you stay informed and gives you an opportunity to raise concerns while the project is being discussed.
