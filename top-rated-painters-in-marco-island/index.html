Questions and preparations before work begins

Before the painting starts, talk through the surfaces involved and what you expect the completed areas to look like. Ask what preparation is planned and how the surrounding parts of the room or home will be kept clean. Make sure you understand which areas are included so you and the painter share the same expectations.

A few useful steps can make that conversation easier:

  • Decide which spaces or exterior sections matter most to you.
  • Note existing paint concerns and show them during the discussion.
  • Gather color preferences, even if they’re still tentative.
  • Ask what needs to be moved or made accessible before work.
  • Raise any questions about the finished appearance before agreeing on a plan.

One common mistake is assuming a repaint is simply a matter of picking a color. The condition and location of the surfaces affect the work to be discussed. Another is waiting until work is about to begin to mention areas you want included. Bring those details up early, and contact a professional if you’re uncertain how to approach a particular surface.
